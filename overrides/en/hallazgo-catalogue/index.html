Hallazgo catalogue — Raquel Costantini

Hallazgo brings together 44 works by Raquel Costantini made between 2018 and 2026. The catalogue follows five movements shaped by walking, observing and gathering signals from the landscape of Cabo de Gata: found forms, eroded matter, remains, stones, plants and small gestures that move from experience into objects without losing their origin. The edition works both as an archive and as a route through the work: it does not explain the landscape, but lets it act on each piece and on the relationship between finding, memory and attention.

The project continues in the Hallazgo 3D castle inside the OOLITA world. You can explore the wider context of the project and follow other editions connected to this body of work.
